# Universal IR libraries

These dictionaries are embedded in firmware and used when an SD override is absent. Existing files under /infrared/assets/ or /infrared/universal/ take precedence; the firmware does not overwrite them.

The original ac.ir, audio.ir, tv.ir and legacy projector.ir were already included with this MeowKit infrared port. The added projectors.ir, leds.ir, fans.ir, bluray_dvd.ir, monitor.ir and digital_sign.ir are from Next-Flip/Momentum-Firmware, revision d3f89dfe2ef6b01839201598e9be1590cba80322, applications/main/infrared/resources/infrared/assets/.
